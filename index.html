About Founder

Dr. Mahaboob Shaik

MBBS, MS (General Surgery), DNB (General Surgery)

Educational and career counsellor, general surgeon, laparoscopic surgeon, and professor of surgery.

Dr. Mahaboob Shaik is a distinguished senior surgeon, published author, and elite medical educator with over 45 years of clinical and academic excellence. He has served as Professor, Head of the Department of Surgery, and Medical Superintendent at premier teaching hospitals including Gandhi Medical College and Malla Reddy Institute of Medical Sciences. He has also contributed nationally as an assessor for the National Medical Council and as a medical advisor to the UPSC Board. Today, through certified counselling work, he brings that same precision to career guidance.
